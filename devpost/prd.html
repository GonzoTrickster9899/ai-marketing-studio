One brief. Two coordinated marketing outputs.

A light, approachable workspace for small-business owners and beginner marketers to create platform-specific social content and a shared seven-day campaign plan.

The user journey

From a blank form or sample business to copy-ready captions and a campaign plan.

One responsive workspace

Desktop uses a form column beside a results panel. Mobile stacks the form first and results below, without a wide table. The user needs no account or analytics dashboard.

Look and feel

Soft off-white canvas, white cards, charcoal text, subtle borders, violet or indigo actions, and readable sans-serif typography. Friendly marketing workspace, not a complex analytics dashboard.

Sample business and first-use empty state

The first-use form is blank. A visible Try sample business action fills in Daily Grind Coffee; iced coffee and matcha latte; college students and young professionals; Promote a special offer; Instagram and Facebook; Friendly; and 10% off selected drinks this weekend. The values stay editable. Until first generation, the results area explains that the campaign will appear after the user completes the brief and generates a plan.

Generated social content

Show one separate content card for each selected platform. Each card has a platform-tailored caption, headline and relevant hashtags, plus its own copy button and copied confirmation. Do not duplicate the same caption across Instagram and Facebook. Use the supplied promotion accurately; never invent facts, prices, discounts, or performance.

Shared seven-day campaign plan

Show one schedule across selected platforms, organized into seven expandable daily sections. Each day contains a content idea, format, captions tailored to each selected platform, a call to action, and a relevant suggested metric. Metrics such as reach, engagement, link clicks, or offer redemptions are tracking suggestions, not measured or predicted results.

Loading, editing, copying, and regeneration
  • On a valid request, show a loading indicator and prevent duplicate submissions.
  • Each selected platform caption can be copied; show a visible confirmation.
  • The user can edit the brief and generate again. A successful new response replaces prior results.
  • When the request fails, stop loading and make the interface usable again.
Request failure and recovery
Plain-language error: explain that generation failed, say the business brief is safe, and offer Try again and Edit brief.
  • Preserve every brief field after failure and reuse the same inputs for Retry.
  • If a previous generation succeeded, keep those results visible and label them as previous results. Replace them only after a new request succeeds.
  • Allow one limited automatic retry for transient failures, then offer manual retry. Avoid repeated automatic requests.
  • For a rate limit, explain the temporary limit and suggested wait. For an invalid response, explain that it could not be processed. If service configuration is missing, show unavailable and do not loop retries.
  • Never expose API keys, internal stack traces, or sensitive server details.

What proves the MVP works?
  • A user can complete the business brief and generate both outputs from it.
  • Each selected platform receives its own tailored caption.
  • The shared seven-day plan gives every day an idea, format, tailored caption(s), call to action, and suggested metric.
  • Copy-to-clipboard works for generated content and confirms the action.
  • Changing the brief and regenerating updates both outputs.
  • First-use empty, validation, loading, and request-failure states are clear.
  • A failed request preserves inputs and previous results; retry and edit actions work.

Boundary

In this POC

Business brief, sample action, Instagram and Facebook captions, one shared seven-day campaign plan, clear states, copy, edit, and retry.

Deferred or excluded

Separate content calendar, marketing chat, broader platform support, authentication, saved history, automated publishing, paid ads, and live analytics.
