PROJECT SCOPE · PROOF OF CONCEPTApproved scope

Marketing help that starts with one business brief.

A beginner-friendly assistant turns a small business’s product, audience, platform, and goal into coordinated social content and a practical campaign plan.

From brief to campaign

One set of details drives both outputs.

Who it helps

A small-business owner or beginner marketer who wants to promote an offer online but may not know how to write social content or structure a campaign. The coffee-shop example makes the first demo concrete.

The distinctive idea

Instead of generating disconnected marketing tips, one business brief produces two coordinated outputs: publishable content and an actionable campaign plan for the same audience, platform, and goal.

Sample journey: coffee shop weekend offer

One minute from input to useful output.

01 · Enter

Business: local coffee shop
Offer: 10% off selected iced coffee and matcha

02 · Choose

Audience: college students and young professionals
Goal: attract weekend customers

03 · Generate

Choose Instagram and Facebook, set a friendly tone, then generate both outputs from the same brief.

04 · Review

Copy separate Instagram and Facebook captions. Review one shared seven-day plan; each day has an idea, format, platform-tailored caption(s), call to action, and relevant metric.

The proof-of-concept boundary

Now · prove the core loop
  • Editable business brief form with product, audience, goal, selected platforms, tone, and offer. Platform support is limited to Instagram and Facebook.
  • AI-generated content with a separate, platform-tailored caption for each selected platform, plus a promotional headline, hashtags, and call to action.
  • One shared seven-day campaign plan from the same brief. Each day has a content idea, format, caption(s) tailored to the selected platform(s), call to action, and relevant metric to track.
  • Clear empty, loading, and error states, plus copy-to-clipboard for generated content.
  • Testable success: generate both outputs from one brief, copy the content, change an input, and generate revised results.
Later · expand the assistant
  • A separate content calendar feature.
  • AI marketing chat and broader content or platform support.
  • Other features from the original concept.
Cut · outside this proof of concept
  • Authentication and saved campaign history.
  • Automated social media publishing and paid advertising integrations.
  • Live analytics. The prototype suggests what to track; it does not connect to platforms or report actual outcomes.
